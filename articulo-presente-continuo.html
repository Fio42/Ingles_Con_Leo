Gramática A1–A2

Presente continuo en inglés

El presente continuo describe algo que está pasando ahora o una situación temporal. Se forma con am/is/are + verbo-ing: I am studying now. Para una rutina, usa el presente simple: I study every day.

Respuesta rápida
  • Ahora: She is cooking.
  • Negativo: They aren't working.
  • Pregunta: Are you listening?
  • Rutina = presente simple; en progreso = presente continuo.

La estructura

AfirmativaNegativaPregunta
I am reading.I am not reading.Am I reading?
She is working.She isn't working.Is she working?
They are waiting.They aren't waiting.Are they waiting?

El auxiliar es el verbo to be; el verbo principal siempre termina en -ing.

Cuándo se usa

Úsalo para acciones en este momento (I'm talking to you) o situaciones temporales (She's staying with her aunt this week). No lo uses para hábitos: I work on Mondays, no I am working on Mondays, salvo que hables de una situación temporal.

Cómo añadir -ing

  • Normal: work → working.
  • Termina en -e: make → making.
  • Consonante-vocal-consonante corta: sit → sitting.
  • -ie cambia a -y: lie → lying.

Errores comunes

❌ She is work now.
✅ She is working now.
❌ I am go to school every day.
✅ I go to school every day.
