Gramática

Who, which, that, where, whose: significado y cómo usarlo

Respuesta rápida

Los pronombres que unen una idea a un sustantivo: who para personas, which para cosas, where para lugares.

Se pronuncia JU / uích

Cómo se usa

Who se usa para personas: The person who called you is my boss. Which es para cosas: The book which I read was long. That puede reemplazar a los dos.

Where se usa para lugares: The house where I grew up was sold. Con which necesitarías una preposición: the house in which I grew up.

Whose muestra posesión: That's the man whose car was stolen.

Ejemplos

The woman who lives next door is a doctor.
La mujer que vive al lado es doctora.
The house where I grew up was sold.
La casa donde crecí se vendió.
That's the man whose car was stolen.
Ese es el hombre al que le robaron el carro.

Error común

❌ The person which called you is my boss.
✅ The person who called you is my boss.

Para personas se usa who, no which.

Truco para recordarlo

Persona: who. Cosa: which o that. Lugar: where. De quién: whose.
